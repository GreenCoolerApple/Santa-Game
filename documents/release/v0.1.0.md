# Santa Game v0.1.0

The first release of Santa Game, a Christmas game made with Kotlin and libGDX for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux, in English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish.

## Downloads

| File | Platform |
|---|---|
| `Santa-Android.apk` | Android |
| `Santa-iOS.ipa` | iOS (unsigned, sign and sideload it with AltStore, Sideloadly, TrollStore or similar) |
| `Santa-Windows.zip` | Windows (extract and run `SantaGame.exe`, Java is included) |
| `Santa-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` | macOS on M-series Macs |
| `Santa-macOS-Intel.zip` | macOS on Intel Macs |
| `Santa-Linux.tar.gz` | Linux (extract and run `SantaGame/bin/SantaGame`, or `install.sh` to add it to the applications menu) |

The macOS app is not notarized: the first time, right-click `SantaGame.app` and choose Open, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine SantaGame.app`.

## Game modes

- **Catch Gifts** — move Santa to catch the falling gifts and dodge the coal. Three lives, and it gets faster the longer you last.
- **Stolen Gifts** — sneak into houses through the fireplace, steal the gifts under the tree and escape without being seen. Families patrol with flashlights, furniture blocks their view and every house is harder. Your run is saved after every house.
- **Escape from the Santa City** — you are a boy lost in a snowy city at night, hunted by green sick people who want to wrap you up in a Christmas present. Find the exit, grab candy and use the minimap. Every city is bigger and faster.
- **Sled Race** — a downhill race against Comet the reindeer, Jingle the elf and Flurry the snowman on a new track every time. Dodge rocks, trees and snowmen, stay out of the deep snow, hit the blue boost pads and collect golden gifts. Your best time is saved.

Every mode has its own game over animation with your stats, new best scores and confetti.

## Menus

- **Intro** — Santa's sleigh crosses the moon before the title drops in. Tap to skip.
- **Main menu** — animated title with Santa's hat, blinking garland, twinkling stars, Santa's sleigh flying by, buttons that pop in with snow on top, and hover effects on desktop.
- **Daily Gifts** — a 15-day calendar on the right of the main menu. Come back every day for a new gift of golden gifts, with bigger rewards on days 5, 10 and 15 (250 golden gifts on the last day). After day 15 the calendar starts again, and a countdown shows when the next gift is ready.
- **Game Mode** — the four modes with your best scores.
- **Awards** — 24 awards in a chalet trophy room, with a progress bar. Four competing elves (Twig, Holly, Biscuit and Sprout) sit at a table drinking hot cocoa, chatting and laughing. Tap them for jokes and funny stories.
- **Shop** — the first visit after starting the game opens with an intro of a snowy chalet whose door swings open. Inside, Pip the elf runs the counter, talks to you and reacts to what you buy. The products are split into Skins, Cosmetics and Perks.
- **Skins** — a chalet dressing room with a big mirror. Switch between Santa and the Boy, see your character in big size and wear any skin you own.
- **Options** — FPS limit, language, music volume, Save Game and Load Game, haptics (mobile only) and Controls (desktop only).

## Golden gifts and Shop

Golden gifts are the game's coins, earned by playing, by scoring well and from the daily gifts.

- **Skins** — five Santa suits (Classic, Frost, Evergreen, Royal and Golden) and four Boy jackets (Blue, Orange, Green and Pink).
- **Cosmetics** — four sled paints (Wooden, Candy Cane, Frost and Golden), shown in Sled Race and on Santa's sleigh in the main menu.
- **Perks** — Extra Life, Quiet Boots and Fast Sneakers.

## Music

Eight original synthesized tracks: the intro, a Christmas waltz for the menus, "Sleigh Dash" for Catch Gifts, "Tiptoe Thief" for Stolen Gifts, "Snowstorm Chase" for Escape from the Santa City, "Downhill Rush" for Sled Race and "Cozy Workshop", a swinging Christmas jazz tune for the Shop. The looping tracks loop seamlessly, crossfade between screens and get quieter when the game is paused.

## Extras

- **Green Talking Apple** — a 50% chance easter egg on the main menu: the dev's apple mascot chats about Christmas, gives you golden gifts and unlocks an award.
- **Pause menu** — a Christmas panel with a garland, holly and a 3-2-1 countdown when you resume. The pause button is shown on mobile; on desktop you use Esc or your Pause key.
- **Haptics** — vibration patterns with adjustable intensity on Android and iOS.
- **Desktop** — a Christmas cursor, F3 screenshots saved to `Pictures/Santa Game`, and a Controls screen to rebind every key.
- **Save system** — a custom binary format with checksums and safe writes, automatic saving and three manual save slots. Loading a slot never removes awards or daily gift progress.
- **Languages** — English, Português (Brasil) and Español, following the system language on first launch.
